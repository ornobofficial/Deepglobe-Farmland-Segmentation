===== HOW TO READ THIS =====
- Pretraining helps: +0.036 test IoU (0.7798 -> 0.8155); same direction on val.
- CE vs CE+Dice: a tie (-0.0015).
- Augmentation: +0.004 test, +0.010 val; within run-to-run noise (~0.01), so a small, not clearly significant gain. It reduced missed farmland (fn 32.2M vs 40.7M).
- Limits: single seed, 120 of 562 train images, 15 epochs, coarse polygon labels.
- Run-to-run noise: the same ResNet34+CE setup gave val IoU 0.7237 in one session and 0.7353 in another.
